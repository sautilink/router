A few steps.
You’re on your way.

Start with a connection to your router. Its own settings page or official app will take you through any changes.

  1. 01
    Join the router's network

    Use Wi-Fi or an Ethernet cable. Mobile data alone cannot reach most local router pages.

  2. 02
    Open the recommended route

    Try the primary gateway, then an alternative. Mesh systems may send you to an official app.

  3. 03
    Use the router's own credentials

    Look at the device label or credentials set by the owner. Never enter them on an unrelated website.

Find your gateway address

Your operating system can show the exact gateway used by the network.

  1. Open your network settings.
  2. Select the connected Wi-Fi network.
  3. Look for Router, Gateway, or Default gateway.

Answers to common questions about finding and opening your router settings.

Visit the Help Centre ›
Can this page detect my exact router model?

No. An ordinary website cannot read your Wi-Fi name or exact router model. We use general browser hints and your confirmed provider or brand to make a transparent recommendation.

Why does the router page fail to open?

Check that you are connected to the router's Wi-Fi, turn off mobile data or VPN temporarily, and try another listed gateway. Some mesh systems work only through their official app.

Is a “not secure” message normal on a local gateway?

Many routers use a local HTTP page or a self-signed certificate. Confirm that the address is a local gateway and never continue if the page sends you to an unfamiliar public domain.

Does SautiLink know my router password?

No. Local gateway pages communicate directly with the router on your network. SautiLink does not request or receive the credentials you enter there.
